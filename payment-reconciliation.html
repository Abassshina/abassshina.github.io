Payment Reconciliation Process Optimization and Automation

Comprehensive Business Analysis Case Study focused on improving reconciliation efficiency, reducing manual effort, strengthening financial controls and enhancing revenue assurance through process optimization and automation.

← Back to Portfolio

Business Problem

Payment reconciliation was largely manual, time-consuming and prone to delays. Transactions originated from multiple channels, creating unmatched records, exception handling challenges and limited visibility into reconciliation performance.

Project Objectives

  • Automate reconciliation activities.
  • Reduce manual intervention and processing time.
  • Improve matching accuracy and exception management.
  • Provide dashboard visibility and reporting.
  • Strengthen financial controls and revenue assurance.

Project Scope

The project covers transaction validation, matching, exception handling, settlement monitoring, reconciliation reporting, dashboard design and process optimization.

Non-Functional Requirements

Success Metrics

  • Reconciliation Rate ≥ 95%
  • Settlement Accuracy ≥ 98%
  • Exception Rate ≤ 5%
  • Reporting Availability = 100%

Risks & Assumptions

  • Data quality issues from source systems.
  • Integration dependencies with payment channels.
  • User adoption and change management requirements.
  • Availability of accurate settlement data.
