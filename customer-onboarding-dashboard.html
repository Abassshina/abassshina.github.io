Customer Onboarding Controls & KYC Efficiency in Digital Banking

Business Analysis Case Study focused on improving customer onboarding efficiency, strengthening KYC controls, reducing onboarding delays, and enhancing executive visibility through dashboard reporting.

Project Overview

Digital banks face increasing pressure to provide a seamless customer onboarding experience while maintaining strong regulatory compliance. This project analyzed the current onboarding process, identified operational and compliance gaps, and designed an optimized future-state framework supported by reporting and dashboard capabilities.

Business Problem

  • Slow customer onboarding process
  • Manual KYC verification activities
  • Customer abandonment during onboarding
  • Limited onboarding visibility
  • Operational inefficiencies
  • Compliance and regulatory risks
